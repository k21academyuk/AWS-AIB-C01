How to use the mock tests

  • Set aside 130 uninterrupted minutes for each test. The timer cannot be paused, and leaving the exam window ends the attempt.
  • After each test, read the explanation for every question you missed, then review the domains marked as focus areas before taking the next test.
  • Aim to score comfortably above 700 on all three tests before you book the real exam.

Practice scores convert your percentage correct to the 100 to 1,000 scale. The real exam uses AWS scaled scoring, so treat your results as a readiness indicator. Last scores are saved in this browser only.

Before you begin

  • The timer starts as soon as you begin and cannot be paused. When time runs out, the exam is submitted automatically.
  • Questions are multiple choice (one correct answer) or multiple response (two or more correct answers; the question says how many). Unanswered questions are scored as incorrect, and there is no penalty for guessing.
  • Use Flag for review to mark questions you want to revisit. The review screen shows every answered, unanswered, and flagged question.
  • Do not leave the exam window. Switching tabs, minimizing the browser, opening another app, exiting full screen, or reloading the page ends your exam immediately and it is scored as submitted.
  • Copy, paste, right-click, and text selection are disabled during the exam. Close other apps and turn off notifications before you start.
